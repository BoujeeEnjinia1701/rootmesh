# BOM notes

`bom.csv` prices one **pilot set**: three sensor stakes and one gateway. Quantities are for the whole set; the notes column gives the count per stake. Rows 1 to 12 match the numbered callouts in `media/exploded.png`. Rows 13 and 14 (gateway and dashboard) are not in the exploded view.

| Group | Indicative cost |
| --- | --- |
| One stake (rows 1 to 12) | about $52 |
| Three stakes | about $156 |
| Gateway (row 13) | about $90 |
| **Pilot set total** | **about $246** (budget $300) |

All prices are indicative 2026 retail estimates in USD for single or small quantities, without shipping or tax. They will be checked against named suppliers at TRL 3. Printed parts are costed as material only. Tools (3D printer, soldering iron, hand auger) are not included.

The module, gateway and cell choices are proposals awaiting Amish (see `docs/REVIEW.md`).
